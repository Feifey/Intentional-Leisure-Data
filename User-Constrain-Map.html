The Cost of Escaping Passive Leisure

Each ring is a constraint. The core costs nothing. Every ring outward costs more to break through. That is why people stay near the centre.
Drag yourself outward to escape the constraints
You broke through every constraint.
It cost energy, time, money, access, social effort, attention, habit-breaking, and fighting cultural pressure. Most people cannot pay all of these at once. That is not a personal failure \u2014 it is a design problem. Intentional leisure becomes possible when these costs are reduced by systems, not willpower.

"The real issue is not only what people want to do, but whether their conditions support it."
