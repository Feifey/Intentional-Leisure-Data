This visualization maps real data on how much people enjoy each leisure activity against how much effort it takes to start — and shows how much time people actually spend on each.

How to read this chart

↑ Y-axis: Enjoyment
How enjoyable people rated each activity on a 1–7 scale.
Source: UK Time Use Survey 2014–15 via Our World in Data
→ X-axis: Effort to start
How much mental activation energy is required to begin the activity (0 = no effort, 100 = very effortful).
Source: Derived from Nature (2025) Effort/Meaning research, normalized 0–100

Data Visualization · Insight 3.1 — Passive Leisure Is the Easiest Option

The Enjoyment Paradox

We default to activities that are easy to start — not activities that make us feel good. This maps real enjoyment scores against real effort levels, with circle size showing actual daily time spent.
